# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Recovery for All a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of recovery pathway, substance, belief, age, disability, gender identity, or lived experience with addiction.

Recovery is not one path. Recovery is the path you build — and that includes how we treat each other while building the tool.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Leading with empathy; assuming good intent
- Respecting anonymity — never press for real names, locations, or using histories
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on the work, not the person
- Using welcoming, recovery-aware language

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Shaming, stigmatizing, or ranking recovery pathways
- Publishing real names, photos, or sobriety details without consent
- Predatory, sexualized, or threatening language
- Trolling, insulting, or harassment
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a recovery setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to **conduct@recoveryforall.app**.

All complaints will be reviewed promptly. Maintainers will respect reporter privacy.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face temporary or permanent repercussions.
