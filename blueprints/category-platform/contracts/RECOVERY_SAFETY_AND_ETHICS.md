# Recovery-Specific Game and Community Safety

This is a supportive voluntary wellness application, not a diagnostic tool, verification authority, clinician, treatment program or sobriety scoreboard.

## Forbidden product mechanics

- Loss of items, levels, relationships or 'pet health' because of relapse, missed days, treatment changes or disclosure of symptoms.
- Pay-to-progress, countdown urgency, variable-ratio randomized rewards for sensitive health actions, artificial streak-preservation purchases, shame labels or public sobriety-duration comparisons.
- Coercive circle/public activity thresholds; 'share to claim reward'; mandatory real-world meeting attendance to advance.
- Verification label for a user-entered meeting log or peer signature without honest confidence/provenance information.
- Requests to upload journal contents, crisis language, sponsor messages or whereabouts for game progression.
- Any Circle quest requiring direct personal information, off-platform contact, in-person peer location or unsafe travel.

## Recovery-centered design alternatives

- Offer equally useful solo, indoor, low-mobility and screen-reader-accessible quest equivalents.
- Rewards for *trying a technique*, reflecting, preparing, or connecting intentionally; never for the content or severity of disclosure.
- Prefer predictable XP, explicit limits and meaningful positive feedback to addictive reward loops.
- Preserve user agency: skip quest, mute avatar, choose no-game view, pause notifications, continue offline without penalty.
- At-risk journeys: if weather or transfer confidence is unsafe/unknown, explain choices and allow an alternative meeting (including online) rather than guilt or missed-quest punishment.
- Protect native/cultural pathways: Wellbriety directory remains culturally informed; never appropriate ceremonies, medicines, sacred practices, or tribal identities as collectible commodities. Consult knowledgeable community advisors before culturally specific game content.

## Verification

Test language with lived-experience reviewers and accessibility reviewers. Capture opt-out, pressure, coercion, trust, perceived safety, comprehension of connectivity, and alternative actions. Observed app retention is **not** clinical proof of recovery effectiveness; prohibit efficacy and patentability claims without independent substantiation.
