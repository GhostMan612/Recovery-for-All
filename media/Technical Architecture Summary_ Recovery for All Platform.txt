Technical Architecture Summary: Recovery for All Platform
1. Platform Vision and Functional Framework
The "Recovery for All" platform is architected as a high-performance digital intervention ecosystem structured around the "6 Dimensions of Wellness." By leveraging local-first processing, the platform addresses these dimensions through a specialized functional framework:
* Occupational Wellness:  Supports personal satisfaction through integrated job readiness resources and access to education insights to align career paths with recovery values.
* Physical Wellness:  Facilitates regular physical activity via a "Recovery Coaches Finder" and access to sober fitness events such as the 25-minute total body workouts or yoga sessions provided by The Phoenix.
* Social Wellness:  Built to foster community engagement through the "Meetings Finder" and peer-led sober social events, including indoor rock climbing and community volunteering, to reduce the isolation common in addiction.
* Intellectual Wellness:  Stimulated through a curated Recovery Resource Library and Research Briefs (sourced from Recovery Iowa), providing users with stimulating mental activities and evidence-based recovery insights.
* Spiritual Wellness:  Facilitated through diverse integration with faith-based and secular paths, including Buddhist Recovery, the Wellbriety Movement (Native American teachings), and Celebrate Recovery.
* Emotional Wellness:  Delivered through AI-driven behavioral interventions that promote self-awareness, stress management, and emotional maturity, allowing users to navigate their feelings without external judgment.

3. Frontend Architecture: Flutter & Cross-Platform Delivery
The frontend is implemented using Flutter to provide a unified UI across mobile platforms. To support high-performance operations, the architecture utilizes a native bridge via the  Android NDK  and  CMake  configurations. This allows the Dart layer to communicate directly with C++ libraries for intensive LLM inference tasks.Key architectural benefits for the user include:
1. Near Real-Time Resource Mapping:  High-speed discovery of recovery resources (AA, SMART Recovery, Women For Sobriety) via the Public Science Collaborative’s "Meetings Finder" API or local GeoJSON processing.
2. Multimodal Service Access:  Low-latency delivery of live chat, crisis hotlines, and Zoom directory access.
3. Performant AI Interaction:  The NDK integration enables the Llama-3.2-3B model to execute with optimized hardware acceleration, ensuring conversational support is available without cloud dependency.
4. Local Data Layer: SQLite and Drift Integration
The persistence layer is built on a local-first architecture using SQLite and the  Drift  library. This handles extensive recovery datasets, including the 512 Alcoholics Anonymous meetings in the Chicagoland area and 113 specialized Chicago support meetings.Architectural Advantages of the Local Data Layer:
* Reactive Streams:  Utilizing  Drift’s reactive streams , the UI updates in near real-time. When meeting data or user history changes, the map and resources reflect updates instantly without requiring manual refreshes.
* Privacy-Centric Persistence:  Sensitive search history and recovery status remain on the hardware, ensuring the device serves as a personal vault for behavioral health data.
* Uninterrupted Access:  Guarantees that critical resource discovery—including Naloxone locations and crisis contacts—remains functional in environments with zero connectivity.
* Performance Optimization:  Eliminates the "round-trip" latency of traditional REST APIs, allowing the LLM to query the local database for RAG operations with millisecond response times.
5. On-Device AI Integration: Llama-3.2-3B LLM
The platform integrates the Llama-3.2-3B model via  Dart FFI (Foreign Function Interface) , allowing the Flutter application to invoke the C++ inference engine compiled through the Android NDK. This "plumbing" ensures that the LLM operates as a native system service.LLM System Components:
* Prompting & Motivation:  Employs evidence-based behavior change strategies focused on goal setting and reinforcement.
* Context Management:  Maintains a stateful understanding of the user’s recovery journey to provide continuity in support.
* Ethical Guardrails:  Specialized safety layers monitor for community-specific nuances. For example, guardrails are configured to recognize that  Narcotics Anonymous (NA) may not accept the use of Suboxone or Methadone . The system is architected to prevent the model from providing specific medication advice while remaining sensitive to these group-specific stigmas to avoid alienating users.

7. Intelligent Retrieval: RAG and Memory Models
To maintain a high degree of personalization on resource-constrained devices, the architecture combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with psychological models of memory."The retrieval architecture focuses on identifying patterns and sustained engagement to ensure the system remains responsive to individual needs as they evolve."The system implements a  decay function  (derived from psychological models of "importance and forgetting"). This prioritizes recent and high-impact user interactions within the Llama-3.2-3B model's limited context window. By mathematically de-prioritizing older, less relevant data, the architecture prevents context clutter while ensuring the model remains grounded in the user's current recovery trajectory.
8. Security, Privacy, and Ethical Guardrails
As the project is supported by  State Opioid Response (SOR) funds through the Iowa HHS and SAMHSA/HHS , the architecture must adhere to the highest standards of "Responsible Implementation."Security Posture:
* 100% Confidentiality:  On-device processing is an architectural requirement to meet federal and state mandates for confidential help. No conversation data regarding substance use or mental health ever leaves the physical hardware.
* Zero-Cloud Vulnerability:  By eliminating the need for centralized LLM servers, the platform is inherently immune to large-scale data breaches that typically target behavioral health repositories.
* User Empowerment:  All sensitive data remains under the user's direct control, ensuring a judgment-free environment that prioritizes the individual's safety and privacy.
